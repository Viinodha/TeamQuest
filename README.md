### find your crew. build your team. start the quest. 🫧

TEAMQUEST is a teammate finder built for college students looking for people to team up with for **hackathons, contests, projects, clubs, research, and more.**

No login. No complicated matching system. Just browse, find someone who fits your quest, and **Team Up.** 👥

---

## 🌱 Why TEAMQUEST?

Hackathons and contests are a pretty big part of college life here, but finding the right people to build a team with can be surprisingly difficult, especially for freshers.

I've seen students on Reddit, college confession pages, and other unofficial communities asking around for compatible teammates for contests and hackathons — both technical and non-technical.
Students were looking for people with very specific skills and interests, but finding them often meant going through endless LinkedIn profiles, checking what projects someone had worked on, figuring out whether they were actually looking for a team, and then trying to contact them.

## 🧩 What can you do with TEAMQUEST?

Find students based on:

-  College, Year & section, Technical skills, Non-technical skills, Past projects, Interests and what they're looking for in a teammate

Found someone who seems like they would fit your team?

Hit **Team Up** to reveal the contact information they've chosen to share and discuss your project further.

TEAMQUEST isn't limited to students from our own campus.
